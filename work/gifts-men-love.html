01 / Brief

USL Diageo's premium spirits portfolio needed a festive gifting campaign across its brands. Because it had to hold across several labels rather than one bottle, the idea had to sit above any single product and speak to the person buying the gift, not the distiller selling it.

The line was #GiftsMenLove: a reframe that asks what the man in question would actually want, and lets the portfolio answer it brand by brand. The campaign leaned on user-generated content, and that is what the jury recognised: Silver at Lighthouse Insights 2017, Best Use of User Generated Content.

02 / Idea

Ask what he'd
actually love.

Most festive gifting work describes the pack. This asked the buyer's question instead: what would he actually love to be given? #GiftsMenLove turned a portfolio of premium spirits into a set of answers, and put the audience's own gifting choices into the campaign rather than a scripted brand story. My copy had to work for several labels at once and still sound like a person, not a press release.

03 / What we made
  • Campaign copy for the festive gifting season across Diageo's luxury spirits portfolio.
  • The #GiftsMenLove framing applied across multiple labels, so the campaign read as one idea rather than several brand messages.
  • UGC prompts that invited people to answer for themselves who the gift was for.
