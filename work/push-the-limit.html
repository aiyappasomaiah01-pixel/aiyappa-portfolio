01 / Brief

Samsonite launched Lite-Shock, a luggage line the brand described as both light and highly indestructible. A durability claim is easy to make and hard to believe, so the objective was to show it in the most unimaginable ways possible rather than describe it. The campaign line set the terms: push the limit.

The campaign ran through influencers and had to land as a launch, not a product update, those are the two things it later won for at Lighthouse Insights 2018. I was the scriptwriter on the digital films, and the deck keeps a separate set of ideas I worked on beyond them.

02 / Idea

Prove it.
Don't say it.

The brief was written as a dare, so the work answered it literally: if there is a limit to what this suitcase survives, find it and push past it. Influencers carried the proof, and #PushTheLimit held the films and the surrounding ideas together. My scripts had one job, to make each scenario feel like something the audience had not watched a suitcase go through before, without losing the product claim.

03 / What we made
  • Scripts for the campaign's digital films, built around the #PushTheLimit premise.
  • Scripts written to keep the product claim in shot: light to carry, and hard to destroy.
  • A separate set of campaign ideas beyond the films, documented alongside them in the deck.
