01 / Brief

Samsonite wanted people to travel more often and to treat their luggage as the companion that makes the trip possible, not an afterthought. The ask was a mid-size campaign with room for the audience to take part, running through summer 2018 rather than as a single burst, so the idea had to hold up across a season of posts.

The insight was that the urge to travel arrives before the plan does, and most people talk themselves out of it. #WhenTravelCalls made answering that urge the point of the campaign, which gave Samsonite a reason to be in the feed without leading with a sale.

02 / Idea

When travel calls,
answer it.

The premise was that travel is a call you answer, not a plan you keep postponing. Samsonite sat inside the moment of deciding to go: travel diaries from Vir Das, Masaba Gupta and Bruised Passports, a carousel of traveller archetypes written to be tagged, and a contest that asked people to post their own trips. I wrote the copy across those formats, keeping one line running through the season.

03 / What we made
  • Copy across the always-on summer platform: travel diaries, carousels, contest posts and captions.
  • Caption and post copy for the travel diaries with Vir Das, Masaba Gupta and Bruised Passports.
  • A tag-bait carousel of traveller archetypes, each one written to point at a friend worth tagging.
  • A user-generated contest that invited people to post their own trips under the platform line.
