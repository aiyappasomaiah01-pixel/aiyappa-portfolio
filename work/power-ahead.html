Great Learning · 2020–2021Power
Ahead

Social series for Great Learning, built on Virat Kohli, an office dinosaur and a cast of superheroes.

Brand
Great Learning
Year
2020–2021
My role
Ideation, research, copywriting
Scope
Social · Series · Celebrity
01 / Brief

Great Learning sells PG programmes to working professionals, and Virat Kohli was the face of that pitch. I worked across its social streams: WFH memes as offices reopened, always-on fixed and fluid posts, motivational posts built on Kohli's training discipline, an Office Dino comic and a superhero brand-hygiene series.

Office Dino carried the sharpest idea: one person in every workspace who camouflages laziness with excuses and antics, written as a comic-book series. The superhero run took the opposite route, profiling Marvel characters as people who upskill. Both needed the same research before a line was written: how the audience talks about work, and where the brand can answer without lecturing.

02 / Idea

Upskilling, sold through
the people who dodge it.

The account did not need another post about career ambition. It needed formats that could run every week without going stale. Each series borrowed something the audience already cared about, a workspace type, a Monday reset, a superhero, and let the upskilling point land inside the joke.

03 / What we made
  • Office Dino: a comic-book series about the colleague who camouflages laziness.
  • WFH memes for the return-to-office moment.
  • Virat Kohli motivational posts, tying his cricket discipline to upskilling.
  • Superhero brand-hygiene posts drawing a parallel between Marvel characters and learning.
  • Fixed and fluid digital posts for the always-on handles.
